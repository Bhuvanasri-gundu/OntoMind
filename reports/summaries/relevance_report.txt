METHODOLOGY
============================================================
Relevance scoring uses three complementary signals:
1. Keyword matching (40% weight)
   - Weighted mHealth keyword presence in title and abstract
   - Title matches weighted higher than abstract matches
2. MeSH term matching (30% weight)
   - Checks MeSH headings against mHealth-relevant terms
   - Papers without MeSH terms receive neutral score (0.5)
3. TF-IDF cosine similarity (30% weight)
   - Computes similarity to mHealth reference description
   - Uses bigrams and normalized TF-IDF vectors

LIMITATIONS:
- Keyword lists may not capture all mHealth subdomains
- MeSH terms are missing for ~50% of papers (neutral scored)
- TF-IDF similarity depends on vocabulary overlap
- Borderline papers (score ~0.3) need manual review
- The system may under-score novel mHealth applications
